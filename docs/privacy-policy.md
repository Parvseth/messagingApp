# Privacy Policy for ONE

**Effective Date:** 2026-08-15

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to ONE ("we," "our," "us"). Your privacy is our priority. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices and the security measures we employ, specifically our use of End-to-End Encryption (E2EE), to ensure that your communication remains strictly confidential.

## 2. End-to-End Encryption (E2EE)
All messages (including text, images, videos, documents, location data, and contacts) sent through ONE are end-to-end encrypted. 
- **What this means:** The content of your communications is encrypted on your device and can only be decrypted by the intended recipient.
- **Our Access:** We cannot read, view, listen to, or access the contents of any messages exchanged between users. The cryptographic keys used to encrypt and decrypt messages are stored exclusively on your device and your pair's device.

## 3. Information We Collect
To provide our service, we collect the minimum necessary information:
- **Phone Number:** Used solely for authentication and account creation via Firebase Authentication.
- **Push Notification Tokens:** Used to alert you of new messages via Expo Push Notification Service.
- **Profile Data:** Display names and profile images, which are only shared with your single paired contact.

## 4. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information only to:
- Authenticate your account.
- Facilitate the pairing process with your chosen contact.
- Deliver encrypted messages to your device.
- Send push notifications for new messages.

## 5. Third-Party Services
We utilize the following third-party services:
- **Firebase Authentication & Firestore:** For account management and as a blind relay for encrypted message payloads.
- **Expo Push Notifications:** For delivering message alerts.
These services do not have access to the decrypted content of your messages.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion
- Encrypted messages may be temporarily stored on our servers until they are delivered to the recipient. Once delivered and acknowledged, they may be deleted from our servers.
- You can request the deletion of your account and associated metadata at any time through the app settings.
